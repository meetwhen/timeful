## Session Objective

Increase the width of the timezone button in the event creation forms (`NewEvent`, `NewSignUp`, `NewGroup`) from ~40px to a fixed 112px, and move the "Timezone" label out of the `TimezoneSelector` component so each form owns its label (placed to the left of the button). Implementation is complete; 4 unit-test assertions fail and the user asked to finish the fix in another session.

## Motivation And Impact

The compact `NewEvent` timezone selector rendered its visible field at `max-content` (~40px) inside a 112px container, so the button was far narrower than intended even though `fixed-width` (112px) was set. `NewSignUp`/`NewGroup` used a 160-256px responsive width and the label lived inside the selector. The selector now renders no label, the button is a true fixed 112px in all three forms, and the label is the form's responsibility (also for the event-page toolbar's "Shown in" label).
