## Important Decisions

- **Stalwart needs an explicit Certificate object.** With `requestTlsCertificate=false` the wizard leaves the TLS store empty; `sync-certs.sh`'s copy + `ReloadTlsCertificates` alone cannot load certs. The one-time object creation (file references into the `/certs` mount) is required; the reload action then re-reads the files on each cert rotation.
- **`defaultCertificateId` must point at the created object's real id** (`jap2vwwgaaqc` in this deploy), not a guessed `b`.
- **CLI reachability**: from a `stalwartlabs/cli` container, `STALWART_URL` must be the Caddy public URL (`https://mail.timeful.fun`); a hardcoded `http://127.0.0.1:8080` fails (container loopback) unless `--network host` is used. Credentials were read from `/srv/mail/.env` inline (no `source`, which failed over the remote `sh`).
- **Nightly cert sync belongs in root's crontab** because Caddy's volume certs are root-only.

## Decision Rationale

- The cert pipeline was already designed around `sync-certs.sh` + Caddy ownership of ACME (see `infra/support-mail/README.md`). The gap was that two VM-specific facts (the `caddy/` mountpoint segment and root-only volume perms) were undocumented; both are now in `.env.example`/README and committed rather than only applied by hand.
