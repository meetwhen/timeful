## Constraints And Assumptions

- `openssl s_client` banner checks on SMTP are unreliable: the banner can arrive after the default read window; always use a full SMTP session (EHLO) or `openssl -starttls smtp` rather than `head` on the raw socket.
- IMAP checks via `imaplib` against `127.0.0.1` need an unverified SSL context (`ssl._create_unverified_context()`), since the cert CN is `mail.timeful.fun`.
