## Important Decisions

- Delete the framework-baseline record and superseded timed-event record; keep
  nine ADRs with contiguous canonical IDs ADR-001 through ADR-009.
- Keep ADR-005 only for civil-date and end-of-day modeling; retain
  working-hours, overlap-construction, and week-selection conventions in
  `frontend/AGENTS.md`.
- `docs/design/README.md` is the repository-wide guide for current and future
  design-record types; it owns the ADR index.
- ADRs may link to primary FRs and QRs they address. Primary FRs and QRs do
  not reference ADRs. FRs and QRs produced by an ADR may declare that
  provenance.
- Use a YAML `derived_from` list for ADR-derived requirements. A future
  mechanically generated trace table provides reverse traceability; ADRs do
  not manually list derived requirements.
- Provenance may name a superseded ADR when that ADR remains the historical
  origin of the requirement.
- Follow up later with an mdsh-based trace-table automation task rather than
  implementing a custom generator in this migration.

## Decision Rationale

Primary requirements remain valid without an implementation choice, while
ADR-derived requirements need auditable origin. `mdsh` supports in-place
Markdown generation and a `--frozen` stale-output check; see
https://github.com/zimbatm/mdsh.
