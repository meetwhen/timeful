## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
ADR-019 currently decides that platform_identities.external_user_id holds the account's 24-character hexadecimal identifier and is the one resolution point. Replace that decision with a new ADR: platform_identities.id, a native UUIDv7, is the account's sole identifier, and the separate external_user_id column is removed. The new ADR decides the canonical wire form and encoding, how guest and unowned zero identity is represented, native uuid storage for the consolidated reference columns, the hard-cutover policy (single guarded backfill, no compatibility lookup, existing sessions force re-sign-in), and the rejected alternatives (keep 24-hex, add a UUIDv7 external_user_id, transitional dual-format support, keep TEXT storage). It supersedes ADR-019, which keeps its history with superseded_by. Update the affected requirement and boundary documentation.

Constraints:
- Follow docs/AGENTS.md sentence-per-line authoring and the ADR frontmatter in docs/design/architecture/adr/TEMPLATE.md.
- Supersede ADR-019 rather than rewriting its decision; ADR-019 keeps its original text plus superseded status and superseded_by.
- Preserve FR-121 traceability and the ADR-018 boundary link in the new record.
- Update FR-059 if its ObjectID-like name rejection depends on the account identifier format.
- Update server/docs/postgres-data-boundaries.md for the consolidated uuid references.
