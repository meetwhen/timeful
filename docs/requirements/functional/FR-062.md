# FR-062: Restore Response-Author Access After Event Sign-In

When a [response author](../../terminology/glossary.md#response-author) proves their browser-local identity and signs in to manage an event, the system shall associate that identity with the authenticated account. On another device, the authenticated account shall be able to edit that author's [protected responses](../../terminology/glossary.md#protected-response) for the event.
