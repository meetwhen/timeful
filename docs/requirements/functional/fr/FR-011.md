# FR-011: Collapse Inactive Timed Grid Runs

On a [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event) page while
viewing or scheduling an event, when [Show all hours](../../../terminology/glossary.md#show-all-hours)
is disabled, the grid shall collapse leading, interior, and trailing inactive
runs of at least three consecutive whole hours.

This requirement does not apply while editing availability or setting specific
times.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Collapse bands align to complete hours.
- The time axis shows each collapsed band's start boundary, including a day boundary such as 00:00 when applicable.
- Scheduling does not expand collapsed bands.
- Calendar, selected, and saved-schedule overlays render as separate visible fragments rather than crossing collapsed rows.
