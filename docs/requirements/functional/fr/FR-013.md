# FR-013: Preserve Timed Slot Instants Across Display Timezone Changes

Changing the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone) shall preserve every [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot)'s [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant) and shall not change the [Event Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-timezone).

## Acceptance Criteria

- A [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot)'s projected date,
  clock label, tooltip date and time, and [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column)
  update for the selected [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone).
- A [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot) renders no more
  than once, including when its displayed time crosses midnight.
- Wrapped ranges remain continuous without structural split gaps.
