# FR-013: Preserve Time Slot Instants Across Display Timezone Changes

Changing the [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone) shall preserve every [Time Slot's](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant) and shall not change the [Event Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-timezone).

## Acceptance Criteria

- A [Time Slot's](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) projected date, rendered clock time, tooltip date and time, and [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) update for the selected [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone).
- A [Time Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) renders no more
  than once, including when its displayed time crosses midnight.
- Wrapped ranges remain continuous without structural split gaps.
