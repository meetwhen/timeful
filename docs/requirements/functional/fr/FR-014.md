# FR-014: Derive Timed Grid Cell States From Event Domains

[Timed Grids](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) shall derive [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots), [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots), and [Padding Cells](../../../terminology/glossary.md#padding-cell) from the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain).

## Acceptance Criteria

- In [Custom Timed Domain Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#custom-timed-domain-editing), [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) are selected, [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots) remain editable, and cells outside the [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain) are non-editable padding.
- On a [Custom Timed Domain Mode](../../../terminology/glossary.md#custom-timed-domain-mode) event page, the read-only grid's time axis covers the saved [Active Slots'](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) extent, falling back to the full [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain) when no **Active Slots** exist.
  The full civil-day axis appears only in [Custom Timed Domain Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#custom-timed-domain-editing) or when the ["Collapse disabled times" Option](../../../terminology/glossary.md#collapse-disabled-times-option) is disabled.
  Turning the option off renders the full civil-day axis on every grid page until it is turned on again.
- On a [Custom Timed Domain Mode](../../../terminology/glossary.md#custom-timed-domain-mode) event page, [Active Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slots) are respondent-selectable and [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots) are not respondent-selectable.
- [Range Timed Domain Mode](../../../terminology/glossary.md#range-timed-domain-mode) grids render the [Active Slot Range](../../../terminology/glossary.md#active-slot-range) with hour-aligned axis boundaries.
- [Inactive Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#inactive-slots) are light grey, and [Padding Cells](../../../terminology/glossary.md#padding-cell) are dark grey.
