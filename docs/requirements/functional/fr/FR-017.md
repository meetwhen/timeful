# FR-017: Preserve Timed Slots Across Daylight-Saving Transitions

A timed-grid cell's identity shall be its instant rather than its displayed clock label.

## Acceptance Criteria

- When a local clock time occurs twice after clocks move backward, the grid renders both slots in chronological order and distinguishes their labels by applicable timezone offset or abbreviation.
- When clocks move forward, the grid does not render nonexistent local times.
- Enabled-domain membership, active selection, [Availability Responses](../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response), drag selection, scheduling, and tooltips preserve slot identity by instant.
