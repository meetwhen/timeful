# FR-017: Preserve Timed Slots Across Daylight Saving Transitions

A [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) cell's identity shall be its [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot)'s [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant) rather than its displayed clock label.

## Acceptance Criteria

- When a local clock time occurs twice after clocks move backward, the
  [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) renders both
  [Timed Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot) in chronological
  order and distinguishes their labels by applicable timezone offset or abbreviation.
- When clocks move forward, the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid)
  does not render nonexistent local times.
- Enabled-domain membership, active selection, [Availability Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response), drag selection, scheduling, and tooltips preserve [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot) identity by [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant).
