# FR-017: Preserve Time Slots Across Daylight Saving Transitions

A [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) cell's identity shall be its [Time Slot's](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant) rather than its rendered clock time.

## Acceptance Criteria

- When a local clock time occurs twice after clocks move backward, the
  [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) renders both
  [Time Slots](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) in chronological
  order and distinguishes their labels by applicable timezone offset or abbreviation.
- When clocks move forward, the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid)
  does not render nonexistent local times.
- [Enabled Domain](../../../terminology/glossary.md#enabled-domain) membership, active selection, [Event Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response), drag selection, scheduling, and tooltips preserve [Time Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot) identity by [Instant](../../../terminology/glossary.md#instant).
