# FR-042: Keep Timed Grid Highlights Within Their Cells

On a [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event) page, an interactive [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot) highlight border shall remain within that slot's cell and shall not overlap neighboring slot borders.
