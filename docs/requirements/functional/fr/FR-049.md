# FR-049: Arrange Mobile Event-Page Controls In Three Rows

On a mobile [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event) page, the [Display Time Format](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-time-format), [Display Timezone](../../../terminology/glossary.md#display-timezone), and number-of-days controls shall appear together in the first row, grouped from the left edge with compact gaps.

Show best times or Show best days shall occupy the second row on its own, and More options shall occupy the third row, left-aligned and only as wide as its text requires.

[Dates-Only Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event) pages shall not show those **Timed Event** controls.
