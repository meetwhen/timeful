# FR-064: Keep Protected Availability Responses In Schedule Overlap

Outside [Blind Availability](../../../terminology/glossary.md#blind-availability), the event page shall include a [Protected Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#protected-response)
that the current [Event Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor)
cannot edit, and shall include its availability in [Schedule Overlap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#schedule-overlap)
views.
