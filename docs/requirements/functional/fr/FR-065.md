# FR-065: Show Availability Responses For Availability Editing

Outside [Blind Availability](../../../terminology/glossary.md#blind-availability), when an [Event Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor) opens [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing), the system shall list every [Availability Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response), including [Protected Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#protected-response) the **Event Visitor** cannot edit.
It shall permit editing only for **Availability Responses** the **Event Visitor** is authorized to edit.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Outside [Blind Availability](../../../terminology/glossary.md#blind-availability), before the [Event Visitor](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-visitor) selects an [Availability Response](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response) subset, the list orders **Availability Responses** owned by the visitor's [Event Guest](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-guest) first, then other [Open Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#open-response), then other [Protected Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#protected-response).
