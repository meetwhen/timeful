# FR-084: Preserve Blind Availability Privacy

For an event using [Blind Availability](../../../terminology/glossary.md#blind-availability), a non-owner [Event Guest](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-guest) shall be able to create and manage multiple [Availability Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response) that the guest is authorized to manage.

The system shall expose to that non-owner only the [Availability Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response) the guest is authorized to manage.
It shall not expose other responses, their availability, their identities, or their count through event payloads, [Availability Editing](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-editing), response selection, or [Schedule Overlap](../../../terminology/glossary.md#schedule-overlap) views.
The [Event Owner](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-owner) shall be able to view every **Availability Response**.
