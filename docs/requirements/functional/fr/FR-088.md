# FR-088: Preserve the Selected Timed Slot and Tooltip When Opening the Mobile Responses Offcanvas

On mobile, opening the `Responses` offcanvas shall preserve the already-selected [Timed Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-slot).

Its visible tooltip shall remain shown during and after the offcanvas opens.
