# FR-088: Preserve The Selected Time Slot And Tooltip When Opening The Mobile Responses Offcanvas

On mobile, opening the `Responses` offcanvas shall preserve the already-selected [Time Slot](../../../terminology/glossary.md#time-slot).

Its visible tooltip shall remain shown during and after the offcanvas opens.
