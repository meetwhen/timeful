# FR-090: Label Every Whole-Hour Timed Grid Line

Every whole-hour horizontal line of the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid) shall display its hour label on the left, including the line at the top of a collapsed-hours strip.

Half-hour lines and [Dates-Only Grids](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-grid) are excluded.
