# FR-091: Present End-of-Day Boundaries Clearly in the Time-Range Picker

In 24-hour mode, the time-range picker shall label hours zero-padded from `00:00` through `23:00`, followed by an [End-of-Day Boundary](../../../terminology/glossary.md#end-of-day-boundary) labeled `24:00`.
In 12-hour mode, hours 1 through 12 keep their 12-hour labels, and the **End-of-Day Boundary** is always labeled `24:00`.
Selecting the **End-of-Day Boundary** shall render as `00:00` in the next [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) of the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid).
