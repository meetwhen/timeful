# FR-091: Present End-of-Day Boundaries Clearly in the Time-Range Picker

In 24-hour mode, the time-range picker shall label hours zero-padded from `00:00` through `23:00`, followed by an end-of-day option labeled `24:00`.

In 12-hour mode, the end-of-day option shall be labeled `12 AM`.

Selecting the end-of-day boundary shall render as `00:00` in the next [Projected Date Column](../../../terminology/glossary.md#projected-date-column) of the [Timed Grid](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-grid).
