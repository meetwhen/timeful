# FR-092: Keep The Legend Section Visible Without Event Responses

When a [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event) or [Dates-Only Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event) has no [Event Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-response), its event-page [Legend Section](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend-section) shall remain visible and show only enabled or active states.

This applies at every viewport and in every grid mode.
