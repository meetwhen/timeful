# FR-092: Keep the Legend Visible Without Availability Responses

When a [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event) or [Dates-Only Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#dates-only-event) has no [Availability Responses](../../../terminology/glossary.md#availability-response), its event-page [Legend](../../../terminology/glossary.md#legend) shall remain visible and show only enabled or active states.

This applies at every viewport and in every grid mode.
