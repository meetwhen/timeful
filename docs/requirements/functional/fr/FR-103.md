# FR-103: Expose The Editable Slot Duration In New-Event Advanced Options

For a [Timed Event](../../../terminology/glossary.md#timed-event), the new-event form shall expose the [Slot Duration](../../../terminology/glossary.md#slot-duration) under `Advanced options`.

The creator shall be able to edit the [Slot Duration](../../../terminology/glossary.md#slot-duration) value; the control shall not be read-only.

Saving shall persist the chosen [Slot Duration](../../../terminology/glossary.md#slot-duration), and the backend shall validate it when [Event Settings](../../../terminology/glossary.md#event-settings) are accepted.
