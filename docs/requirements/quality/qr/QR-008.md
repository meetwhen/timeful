# QR-008: Support Accessible Coordination Flows

When a user creates an event, views an event, or submits availability on a
supported desktop or mobile browser, the frontend shall conform to WCAG 2.2 AA
for those flows.

The response measure is passing automated accessibility checks and documented
manual keyboard and screen-reader checks for each flow before release. Known
exceptions shall be recorded with their affected flow and remediation status.
