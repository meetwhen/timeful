# QR-011: Support Large Coordination Workloads

## Source

Five hundred concurrent active users access events.

## Stimulus

Users view events or save availability under a representative workload.

## Environment

Timed and days-only event workloads contain 500 stored responses, and users either access one shared event or are distributed across events.

## Artifact

Event coordination workload capacity.

## Response

The system shall support the workload without operation failures caused by capacity exhaustion.

## Response Measure

The workload completes without operation failures caused by capacity exhaustion and continues to meet the response-time limits in [QR-009](QR-009.md) and [QR-010](QR-010.md).
