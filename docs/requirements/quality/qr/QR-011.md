# QR-011: Support Large Coordination Workloads

The system shall support timed and days-only event workloads with 500 stored
responses and 500 concurrent active users, both when all users access one
shared event and when they are distributed across events.

The response measure is that the workload completes without operation failures
caused by capacity exhaustion and continues to meet the response-time limits in
QR-009 and QR-010.
