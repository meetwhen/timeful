The override was introduced by `cf3600ea` (Jun 15) and never accounted for groups; pre-refactor `fromRawEvent` spread `raw` (type passed through).
- Fix: skip the override when `raw.type === eventTypes.GROUP` (added `eventTypes` import). Regression test first in `transportBoundaries.regressions.test.ts` ("preserves the group type for canonical timed weekly payloads") — RED then GREEN.
