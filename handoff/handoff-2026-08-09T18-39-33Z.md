## Important Decisions

**Settled canonical timed-slot contract (product truth, governs all remaining work):**
- `enabled slots` = **always the full civil day** (00:00–24:00, *event timezone*) of each picked date (specific-dates) / recurrence anchor-week instance day (weekly). Derived, never persisted, never transported. (Aligns with `glossary.md` and the derived-only model from `handoff/handoff-2026-08-09T12-31-01Z.md`.)
- `active slots` = only what is selected in the specific-times editor; the only persisted slot set. Range-created events: initial actives = the range — but their **enabled domain is also full-day**. Create-with-specific-times: empty active subset (existing approved create contract).
- **Wipe rule**: on save, any active instant outside the enabled domain (full day of picked dates, event timezone) is dropped — *intended*, e.g. the cross-midnight Jan-6 UTC instants of a Jan-5-picked UTC event.
- **Added dates**: pick a new date → enabled-only, **no actives** (no full-day auto-activation).
- **Timezone change**: keep the **re-anchor** behavior (previous session's fix): picked dates move to the actives' new-local dates so instants survive (`specificTimesEditDraft.ts` `reanchoredMembershipDays`, spec test 2 remains green). Exception to the wipe rule.
- **Event page rendering**: the read-only grid **collapses bands without actives** (no need to always show the full day); the saved slot-generation window is the active-range metadata (kept intact).
- Documentation of the contract is a deliverable: amend **ADR-012** (slot-generation, advanced-slot-editing, picked-date, timezone, and rendering sections) + glossary (retire "range-generated versus full-day") + check FR-026 wording.
